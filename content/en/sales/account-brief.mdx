```text title="Account briefing prompt"
Prepare a pre-meeting brief for [account name] using work sources I am authorized to access. Include account context, recent interactions, agreed actions, open topics, and suggested meeting questions. Cite each source and date; distinguish facts from inferences; exclude unrelated or sensitive information.
```
