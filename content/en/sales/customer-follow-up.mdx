```text title="Customer follow-up prompt"
Using the meeting notes below, draft a follow-up email to [customer name]. Recap three agreed points, list actions with owners and due dates, and call out open questions. Keep it professional, warm, and under 180 words. Do not add commitments or information that is not in the notes.

Notes: [paste notes approved for sharing]
```
