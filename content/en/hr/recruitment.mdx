  <p slot="context">The recruiting team needs a consistent question set for a role, based on agreed evaluation criteria.</p>

  <p slot="outcome">Produce a reviewable draft that stays focused on job-related competencies.</p>

  <div slot="prompt">

```text title="Interview preparation prompt"
Using the attached job description and competency framework, suggest eight structured interview questions for [role title]. For each question, state the competency assessed and what evidence a strong answer should include. Do not ask about protected personal characteristics or information unrelated to the job. Flag unclear criteria for the recruiting team to confirm.
```

  </div>

  <p slot="prepare"><strong>Before using.</strong> Review the questions against current recruiting policies and applicable law before use.</p>
